# Decisions and deviations from the plan

- mediapipe 0.10.14 and torch 2.5.1+cpu installed as planned. `kaggle` was installed, then removed (see below).
- **No Kaggle.** The team has no Kaggle account, so Phase 2 was redone with sources that need no login. Nothing is made up: every metric comes from real recordings.
- **Words: ASL Citizen instead of Kaggle ASL Signs.** Microsoft's ASL Citizen (83,399 webcam videos, 2,731 signs, 52 signers) from `https://download.microsoft.com/download/b/8/8/b88c0bae-e6c1-43e1-8726-98cf5af36ca4/ASL_Citizen.zip`. The zip supports HTTP range requests, so only the CSV splits are read up front and only the ~3,100 clips for the chosen ~100 words are streamed later. About 30 clips per sign. Splits are the official signer-disjoint ones: train 35 signers, val 6, test 11.
- Because there is no Kaggle parquet, landmarks are extracted from the video by us with the same MediaPipe `.task` models the browser uses (hand, pose lite, face), so training matches live input. Phase 3c changes accordingly: `build_words_dataset.py` streams a clip, extracts, deletes the clip.
- **License (ASL Citizen, Microsoft Research License):** non-commercial research only, no sharing or distributing the data or modifications of it, and personal data must be destroyed when the research ends. So: raw videos are never kept; `data/` stays gitignored; reference animations built from the signers' landmarks (`web/static/signs/`) are gitignored and generated locally instead of committed; trained weights and metric reports are committed (flagged as a judgement call).
- Word vocabulary is re-derived from ASL Citizen gloss names (e.g. `HELLO`, `DOG1`), one gloss per English word (the variant with most clips). The Kaggle-specific labels in the plan (`minemy`, `hesheit`, `callonphone`, ...) are dropped.
- **Letters:** `Marxulia/asl_sign_languages_alphabets_v03` (10,873 images, A to Z, no signer IDs). Its test split is NOT signer-independent and the report says so.
- **Digits:** ASL Citizen has `ZERO` and `ONE` to `NINE` except `FIVE`. Digit 5 (and extra 1 to 9 images) comes from `Vedant3907/Numbers-Sign-Language-Dataset` (label `10` dropped). Frames from the ASL Citizen clips add signer diversity for 0 to 9 without 5.
- **Emotion:** FER2013 only (`Aaryan333/fer2013_train_publicTest_privateTest`, parquet, 35k, 48x48 grey; the `Jeneral/fer-2013` copy was rejected because its `.pt` files are pickles that cannot be loaded safely). RAF-DB and AffectNet mirrors are skipped because their original licenses forbid redistribution. The plan's target of macro-F1 >= 0.70 on RAF-DB cannot be measured; the report states FER2013 test results and that FER2013 is low resolution and noisy.
